What Your Donation Builds

Every dollar goes toward purchasing the land and establishing our 6 self-sustaining community pillars in Western Australia.

Pillar 1 & 2

Full-Service Masjid & Janāzah Care

Accommodating men, women, youth, seniors, and people with special needs, plus high-standard funeral & janāzah facilities.

Pillar 3

Qur’an & Islamic Education

Modern interactive classrooms for children, teens, adults, sisters, and new Muslims from basic to advanced levels.

Pillar 4

Indoor Sports Complex & Youth Hub

Soccer, basketball, racket sports, martial arts, separate gyms for men and women, and youth leadership camps.

Pillar 5 & 6

Crisis Accommodation & Counselling

Purpose-built consultation rooms for marriage support, youth counselling, Hijama wellness, and urgent crisis accommodation.
